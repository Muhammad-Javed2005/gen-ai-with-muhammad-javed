## **Definition**
Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, servers, networks, programs, and data from digital attacks, unauthorized access, damage, or theft. It involves technologies, processes, and controls designed to safeguard systems and information from cyber threats.

## **Explanation**
Cyber security focuses on defending against cyber attacks, which can come from hackers, viruses, malware, ransomware, phishing, or other malicious activities. It ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA) of data.

Key areas in cyber security include:
- **Network Security** (protecting networks from intruders)
- **Information Security** (securing data from unauthorized access)
- **Application Security** (protecting software from threats)
- **End-user Security** (training users to avoid security risks)
- **Disaster Recovery** (restoring systems after an attack)

## **Simple Example**
Imagine you have an online bank account. Cyber security helps prevent hackers from stealing your money by:
- Using strong passwords
- Enabling two-factor authentication (2FA)
- Encrypting your data
- Installing antivirus software

If someone tries to hack your account, cyber security measures block their access and alert you of suspicious activity.

## **Role in IT Industry**
Cyber security is **critical** in the IT industry because:
- **Prevents Data Breaches** (protects sensitive customer data)
- **Ensures Business Continuity** (keeps systems running smoothly)
- **Complies with Laws** (meets legal & regulatory requirements)
- **Builds Trust** (customers rely on secure systems)
- **Combats Cyber Crime** (reduces financial & reputational losses)

Every company—from small businesses to big corporations—needs cyber security professionals (like ethical hackers, security analysts, and CISOs) to stay safe.

## **Key Points**
✅ **Goal:** Protect data, systems, and networks from cyber threats.
✅ **Types of Threats:** Viruses, phishing, ransomware, DDoS attacks, insider threats.
✅ **Best Practices:**
   - Use strong passwords & 2FA
   - Keep software updated
   - Backup data regularly
   - Train employees on security awareness
   - Install firewalls & antivirus software
✅ **Future Trends:** AI-driven security, zero-trust models, quantum encryption.
